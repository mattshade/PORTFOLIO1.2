# Motion

Motion is **functional**: it orients attention, confirms interaction, and preserves performance. There are no decorative page-wide animations in the baseline system.

## Timing & easing

| Pattern | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Entrance** | `fadeUp` — short translate + opacity; used for hero and section reveals. |
| **Hover (links)** | `0.2s` with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` — snappy, not bouncy. |
| **Grid pulse** | Slow ambient pulse on the fixed background grid — low amplitude so it never competes with content. |

Easing tokens are defined alongside `@keyframes` in `src/index.css` (e.g. `fadeUp`, `gridPulse`, `float`, `glow`).

## Principles

- **Prefer opacity and transform** — avoid animating `box-shadow` or `filter` on large layers during scroll.
- **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`:** critical paths should still work when motion is reduced (the origami aviary background and decorative loops should not block comprehension).
- **Modal & overlay:** project detail uses backdrop + dialog; focus trap is handled in the component—keep durations under **300ms** for open/close.

## What not to do

- No autoplaying parallax on primary reading columns (site-wide scroll perspective lives in `OrigamiAviaryBackground`, behind content).
- No staggered list animations that delay access to resume or contact actions.
