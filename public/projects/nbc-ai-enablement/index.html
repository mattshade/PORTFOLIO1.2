Case study

AI Enablement · NBC News Group

Executive-ready visibility and delivery for Data AI & Emerging Tech: a weekly rhythm of progress, governance, and hands-on enablement across newsroom, product, and engineering.

Strategy Custom agents Training Platforms

Executive summary

I led AI enablement for NBC News Group with a focus on clarity and execution: turning a broad portfolio of pilots and partnerships into something stakeholders could see, prioritize, and ship. That meant pairing weekly progress reporting with structured delivery mechanics—OKRs, backlog hygiene, and explicit stage tracking—so work stayed aligned to outcomes, not just activity.

Operating model

  • Weekly cadence: A consistent report format for leadership—what moved, what blocked, and what was next across agents, training, and platform initiatives.
  • OKRs & prioritization: Helped define outcome-driven OKRs for AI enablement and capabilities, connecting tool adoption and applied research to measurable newsroom and enterprise use cases.
  • Execution hygiene: Drove translation of AI work into structured, scoped Jira stories—improving visibility, ownership, and prioritization across teams.

Portfolio highlights

Custom Copilot & agent programs. Advanced initiatives for editorial, standards, legal, audiences, and on-air production—including story discovery, standards guidance, legal resource access, booking and scheduling workflows, and creative tooling with partners such as Adobe and Figma.

Training & office hours. Hands-on programs spanning ChatGPT office hours, GitHub Copilot training with Microsoft, Google Cloud AI topics (e.g. Gemini, Vertex, agent patterns), and cohort-style enablement so teams could apply AI to real workflows—not slide decks alone.

Platform & data foundations. Progress toward a unified Data Hub mental model—helping teams discover and evaluate datasets with clearer documentation and tiers—alongside pragmatic license and tooling coordination across the generative AI stack.

Impact

The work established a repeatable pattern: transparent progress, governed delivery, and scaled enablement—so newsroom and engineering teams could adopt generative AI faster, with clearer ownership and safer enterprise practices.
